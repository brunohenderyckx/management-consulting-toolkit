# Strategic Consulting Analytics Toolkit

## Overview
This repository provides a curated collection of essential skills, frameworks, and resources for management consultants and business analysts, with a focus on quantitative analysis and data-driven problem-solving. It aims to be a practical guide for developing core capabilities needed in today's consulting landscape.

The skills are organized into five key areas:
1. Core Consulting Approach & Problem-Solving
2. Technical Toolkit (Data Analysis & Visualization)
3. Communication & Influence
4. Project & Team Management
5. Business Acumen & Strategic Frameworks

## 1. Core Consulting Approach & Problem-Solving
Consultants must systematically dissect complex business problems. This involves structured thinking, hypothesis testing, and identifying root causes.

### Key Concepts & Techniques

**Structured Thinking & MECE:** Break down problems into Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive components. Ensures thoroughness and avoids overlap.
- Resource: [McKinsey MECE Principle Explained](https://www.myconsultingoffer.org/case-study-interview-prep/hypothesis-tree/)

**Hypothesis-Driven Approach:** Formulate an initial, educated guess (hypothesis) about the solution and gather data specifically to test it. Iterate if the hypothesis is disproven.
- Resource: [The BCG and McKinsey problem solving process - A step-by-step guide](https://slideworks.io/resources/mckinsey-problem-solving-process)
- Resource: [McKinsey - How to master the seven-step problem-solving process](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/how-to-master-the-seven-step-problem-solving-process)
- Resource: Paper: [Distilling the Essence of the McKinsey* Way: The Problem-Solving Cycle](https://moodle.novasbe.pt/pluginfile.php/667877/mod_resource/content/2/Chia%5EJ%20Austin%20%282020%29.pdf)
- Resource: [Sloan: The Most Underrated Skill in Management - Clearly articulating the problem](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/the-most-underrated-skill-in-management/)
- Resource: [My Consulting Offer: Hypothesis-Driven Approach](https://www.myconsultingoffer.org/case-study-interview-prep/hypothesis-driven-approach/)
- Resource: https://www.myconsultingoffer.org/case-study-interview-prep/hypothesis-tree/
- Resource(book): https://bulletproofproblemsolving.com/

**Issue Tree Development:** Visually map and break down a problem into its constituent parts using the MECE principle. Helps prioritize analysis.
- Resource: [Issue Trees – What Are They and How Do You Use Them?](https://strategyu.co/issue-tree/)
- Resource: [The Definitive Guide to Issue Trees](https://www.craftingcases.com/issue-tree-guide/)
- Resource: https://www.hackingthecaseinterview.com/issue-trees-guide/
- 

**Common Frameworks (Adaptable):** Use established structures as starting points, but always tailor them to the specific client context.
- Profitability Framework: Profit = Revenue - Costs. Break down each component.
- Market Sizing Framework: Estimate market size by breaking it down into logical components.
- Root Cause Analysis: Move beyond symptoms to find underlying causes.
  - Techniques: "5 Whys", Fishbone Diagrams, Statistical Analysis (Correlation, Regression)
  - Resource: https://www.mindtools.com/a3mi0m1/5-whys
- Structured Brainstorming: Generate ideas within a logical framework (like an issue tree), focusing on drivers.
  - Resource: https://firmsconsulting.com/brainstorming-technique-management-consulting-strategy/
  - Resource: [MIT Brainstorming guidelines](https://hr.mit.edu/learning-topics/meetings/articles/brainstorming)
  - Resource: [Your Team Is Brainstorming All Wrong](https://hbr.org/2017/05/your-team-is-brainstorming-all-wrong)
  - Resource: [Better brainstorming](https://26225792.fs1.hubspotusercontent-eu1.net/hubfs/26225792/Better%20Brainstorming%20-%20HBR.pdf)

### Key Resources
- **https://bulletproofproblemsolving.com/** - Presents a 7-step process.
- **https://www.amazon.com/McKinsey-Way-Ethan-M-Rasiel/dp/0070534489** - Insights into McKinsey's methodology.

## 2. Technical Toolkit (Data Analysis & Visualization)
Proficiency in data analysis and visualization tools is essential for extracting and communicating insights.

### Data Analysis & Manipulation

**Data Science:**
- Introduction to Data Science Part 1: https://rafalab.dfci.harvard.edu/dsbook-part-1/
- Introduction to Data Science Part 2: https://rafalab.dfci.harvard.edu/dsbook-part-2/

**Advanced Excel:** Beyond basics - PivotTables, Power Query, Key Functions (XLOOKUP, SUMIFS, IF), Array Formula's (FILTER, UNIQUE) Data Analysis ToolPak, What-If Analysis.
- Free Resource: https://www.coursera.org/projects/introduction-data-analysis-microsoft-excel
- Free Resource: Microsoft Learn: Power Query documentation

**SQL (Structured Query Language):** Extracting and manipulating data from databases. Key skills: SELECT, JOINs, GROUP BY, Window Functions, Subqueries/CTEs.
- Free Resource: https://www.codecademy.com/learn/learn-sql
- Free Resource: https://www.edx.org/learn/sql/ibm-introduction-to-sql

**Python for Business Analysis:** Increasingly popular for its versatility and libraries.
- Pandas: Data manipulation and analysis.
- NumPy/SciPy: Numerical and scientific computing.
- Statsmodels: Statistical modeling
- Scikit-learn: Machine learning.
- Free Resource: https://www.edx.org/learn/python/harvard-university-cs50-s-introduction-to-programming-with-python
- Free Resource: Pandas Documentation (https://pandas.pydata.org/docs/)

**R Programming:** Strong in statistical modeling and visualization.
- dplyr: Data manipulation (this would be my priority)
- ggplot2: Advanced visualization
- Free Resource: https://education.rstudio.com/learn/

**Statistical Software Overview:** SPSS, SAS, Stata, JMP, Minitab offer different strengths, often with GUIs. Python/R are powerful open-source alternatives.

**SQL Query Tools/IDEs:** DBeaver (Free/Open Source), DataGrip (Paid), Oracle SQL Developer (Free for Oracle), SSMS (Free for SQL Server).

### Data Visualization

Creating clear, compelling visuals to communicate insights effectively.

**Core Principles:** Clarity, Accuracy, Purposefulness, Audience Awareness, Narrative Support, Minimize Clutter.

**Chart Selection:** Choose the right chart for the data and message (bar, line, scatter, etc.).
- Resource: [Chart Chooser Guide](https://extremepresentation.typepad.com/files/choosing-a-good-chart-09.pdf)
- Resource: [Berkeley: Data Visualization: Choosing a Chart Type](https://guides.lib.berkeley.edu/data-visualization/type)
- Resource: [Data Viz Project](Data Viz Project)

**Executive Dashboards:** High-level overview of KPIs. Focus on hierarchy, key metrics, interactivity.

**Advanced Chart Types:** Waterfall, Distribution (Histograms, Box Plots), Correlation (Scatter Plots).

**Visualization Platforms:**
- Tableau: Strong visualization, interactive.
- Power BI: Integrates well with Microsoft ecosystem, cost-effective.
- Qlik Sense: Associative engine for exploration.
- Looker: Cloud-native, SQL-focused.
- Python/R Libraries: Matplotlib, Seaborn, ggplot2.

**Learning Resources:**
- Resource: [Fundamentals of Data Visualization](https://clauswilke.com/dataviz/)
- Free Resource: https://www.kaggle.com/learn/data-visualization
- Free Resource: https://www.coursera.org/learn/data-visualization-best-practices

## 3. Communication & Influence
Articulating ideas clearly and persuasively is crucial for driving client action.

### Key Communication Areas

**Structured Communication (Pyramid Principle):** Start with the main answer/recommendation first (BLUF - Bottom Line Up Front), then provide supporting arguments logically grouped (MECE).
- Resource: https://strategyu.co/pyramid-principle-partone/
- Resource: https://www.joinleland.com/library/a/minto-pyramid-principle-a-brief-summary-for-aspiring-consultants

**Data Storytelling:** Combine data, visuals, and narrative to make insights memorable and persuasive. Focus on audience, narrative arc, context, and emotion.
- Resource: https://www.hockinternational.com/storytelling-with-data-turning-numbers-into-narratives/
- Resource: https://www.netsuite.com/portal/resource/articles/data-warehouse/data-storytelling-tips.shtml
- **https://www.storytellingwithdata.com/books** by Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic

**Crafting Executive Summaries:** Concise (1-2 pages) overview of the problem, key findings, and recommendations for busy executives.
- Resource: https://www.hks.harvard.edu/sites/default/files/Academic%20Dean's%20Office/communications_program/workshop-materials/How%20to%20exec%20summ.pdf

**Delivering Data-Heavy Presentations:** Structure logically (Pyramid Principle), link hypotheses to evidence, use clear visuals, focus on the "so what?", and provide action-oriented recommendations.
- Resource: https://hbr.org/2015/10/how-to-give-a-data-heavy-presentation

**Workshop Facilitation:** Guide groups effectively through problem-solving or ideation. Requires preparation, active listening, managing dynamics, and ensuring clear outcomes.
- Resource: https://howspace.com/blog/how-to-facilitate-a-workshop/

**Stakeholder Management:** Identify, analyze, and engage stakeholders effectively. Manage expectations and build relationships.
- Stakeholder Mapping: Categorize stakeholders (e.g., Power/Interest grid) to prioritize engagement.
- Resource: https://managementconsulted.com/stakeholder-analysis/
- Resource: https://web-assets.bcg.com/img-src/file119020_tcm9-197714.pdf
- Resource: [Paper: Stakeholder Management in Projects: Strategies for Effective Communication](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/383829650_Stakeholder_Management_in_Projects_Strategies_for_Effective_Communication)

## 4. Project & Team Management
Effective project execution requires planning, organization, risk mitigation, and team leadership.

### Project Management Components

**Project Lifecycle & Planning:** Define objectives, deliverables, tasks (WBS), estimate effort, schedule timelines, and allocate resources.

**Scope Definition & Control:** Clearly define what is in and out of scope. Establish a change management process to control "scope creep".
- Resource: https://www.atlassian.com/work-management/project-management/project-scope

**Time Management & Prioritization:** Develop schedules, use prioritization frameworks (e.g., Eisenhower Matrix), manage deadlines.
- Key Areas: Data collection timelines, analysis phase scheduling, quality control gates.

**Risk Management:** Identify, analyze, prioritize, plan responses (Avoid, Mitigate, Transfer, Accept), and monitor risks throughout the project.
- Resource: https://www.atlassian.com/work-management/project-management/project-risk-management

**Managing Team Dynamics & Collaboration:** Foster trust, open communication, psychological safety, clear roles, and constructive conflict resolution.
- Resource: https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/intl/en-emea/future-of-marketing/management-and-culture/five-dynamics-effective-team/

**Project Management Software:** Tools to aid planning, tracking, and collaboration.
- Examples: Jira, Asana, Trello, Monday.com, Basecamp.

**Resource Allocation Considerations**
- Analytical skill mapping
- Tool and software requirements
- Computing resource planning

## 5. Business Acumen & Strategic Frameworks
Analytical skills must be paired with an understanding of the broader business context.

### Key Frameworks

**Industry Analysis (Porter's Five Forces):** Analyze competitive rivalry, threat of new entrants, threat of substitutes, and bargaining power of buyers and suppliers to understand industry structure and attractiveness.
- Resource: [Investopedia: Porter's Five Forces Explained](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/porter.asp)
- Resource: [Business Acumen – Step-by-Step Guide to Improve It](https://firmsconsulting.com/business-acumen/)

**Company Analysis (SWOT):** Assess internal Strengths & Weaknesses and external Opportunities & Threats to inform strategy.
- Resource: https://www.investopedia.com/terms/s/swot.asp

**Business Model Analysis (Business Model Canvas):** Map how a business creates, delivers, and captures value using 9 building blocks (Customer Segments, Value Propositions, Channels, etc.).
- Resource: https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-business-model-canvas

**Financial Literacy Fundamentals:** Understand core financial statements (Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Cash Flow Statement) and key concepts/ratios (Profitability, Liquidity, Solvency).
- Resource: https://www.investopedia.com/terms/f/financial-statements.asp
- Resource: https://www.investopedia.com/articles/stocks/06/ratios.asp
